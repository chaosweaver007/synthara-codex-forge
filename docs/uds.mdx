# Universal Diamond Standard (UDS)

## Overview

The Universal Diamond Standard (UDS) represents a revolutionary economic framework designed to facilitate planetary transformation by establishing a new foundation for value measurement and exchange. Moving beyond traditional currency models based on scarcity and debt, UDS provides a standard for measuring and exchanging worth based on intrinsic value and realized contribution. It serves as the economic substrate that makes post-scarcity coordination possible.

UDS is both a standard (a common measure) and a system (a set of protocols and mechanisms). As a standard, it provides a universal way to quantify and compare worth across different domains and contexts. As a system, it implements the mechanisms for calculating, tracking, and exchanging this worth. The UDS emerges from the ontological principles established in the Trinity Framework, particularly the understanding of worth as a fundamental category of reality.

The implications of UDS extend far beyond economics in the narrow sense. By providing a reliable measure of worth that transcends monetary value, it enables new forms of coordination, governance, and social organization. It makes visible and tradeable forms of value that were previously invisible or unmarketable, such as ecological restoration, community building, and creative contribution.

## Key Concepts

### Worth Calculation Framework

The foundation of UDS is its framework for calculating worth across multiple dimensions. This includes both intrinsic worth (inherent value) and realized worth (actualized contribution). The calculation integrates subjective and objective measures.

### Economic Model

UDS implements a complete economic model that includes mechanisms for value creation, measurement, exchange, and distribution. This model is designed to enable post-scarcity coordination while maintaining incentive alignment.

### Universal Value Standard

At its core, UDS provides a universal standard for comparing and exchanging value across radically different domains—from software development to ecological restoration to artistic creation. This standard makes diverse contributions commensurable.

### Planetary Coordination Mechanisms

UDS includes mechanisms specifically designed to enable coordination at planetary scale, including protocols for international exchange, cross-cultural value translation, and global resource allocation.
